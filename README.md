# Dark Entity T5 Zombies Mod Menu

A Plutonium Black Ops 1 (T5) zombies mod menu for custom games and private lobbies. Made by UserDarkCloud.

**Disclaimer:** This menu is for custom games only

## Included

- `DarkEntityT5`: the mod menu script

## Features

- **Player:** God Mode, Noclip, Noclip Speed, Infinite Ammo, Zombies Ignore Me, Max Money, +10,000 Points, Refill All Ammo, Super Speed, Unlimited Sprint, No Fall Damage
- **Money:** +15,000 up to +666,000 Points, plus Max Money
- **Weapons:** Wonder Weapons, Assault/SMG/LMG, Pistols, Melee, Shotguns/Snipers/Launchers, and Pack-a-Punched versions of most guns. Also Take Current Weapon.
- **Perks:** includes "Perkaholic" (gives all perks)
- **Zombies:** Kill All Zombies, Set/Turn Off Zombie Position, Open All Doors, Zombie Counter, Timer, Turn On Power, Fast Restart Map, End Game, and more
- **Powerups:** Has All
- **Map Mods:** Third Person, Low Gravity, Money Rain, Double Jump, Super Jump, Teleport Gun, Death Aura, Force Field, Freeze Zombies, Game Speed, and more
- **Round Menu:** jump to rounds 15 through 9999, or skip +1 round
- **Fun:** Long Knife Range, Rapid Fire / Reload, Hit Markers, Rainbow HUD, and more
- **Visions:** Default, Black & White, Pentagon, Theater, Black Screen, RGB Screen, and more
- **Graphics:** No Fog, Fullbright, Hide HUD, Show FPS, Hide Gun, Crosshair Style/Color, Draw Distance, Cap FPS 60
- **Aimbot:** Aim Trigger (ADS or Fire), Aim Bone, Aim Range, Visible Targets Only
- **Teleport:** Map Locations, Save Locations (up to 30 custom spots), My Locations, Print My Position
- **All Players:** Send Nuke, Ammo Box, Double Points, Insta Kill, Fire Sale, or Free Perk to other players; TP to player or to you; Give / Take Mod Menu
- **Messages:** animated banner messages

## Requirements

- Plutonium T5 (Black Ops 1) installed and working
- Launched at least once so the storage folders exist
- WinRAR or 7-Zip, for your download

## Installation

1. Download `DarkEntityT5`
2. Press `Win + R`, type `%localappdata%`, and press OK.
3. Go to `Plutonium` → `storage` → `t5` → `raw` → `scripts` → `sp`. Create any missing folders along the way.
4. Copy `DarkEntityT5` into the `sp` folder.
5. Launch Plutonium T5 and start a zombies game.

**Note:** The menu is host-only by default. Other players need the host to give it to them through All Players → player → Give / Take Mod Menu.
